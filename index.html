Working AI Guidelines 2026 — a training course for Faculty of Education staff

This course turns the Faculty's Working AI Guidelines into twelve short, interactive modules. Each one takes 10–20 minutes and ends with something concrete: a checklist, a glossary, a decision map. Work through them in order, or jump to what's relevant to your role.

How to use this course

1. Read your tier

The coloured strip at the top of every page shows which governance tier(s) — institutional, Faculty, or course/Department — that module speaks to.

2. Interact, don't skim

Flip the definition cards, sort the examples, click through the governance map. The interactions are where the guideline actually sinks in.

3. Keep the toolkit open

Module 12 has the disclosure checklist and citation formats you'll reuse every semester.
