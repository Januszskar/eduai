Purpose, Scope & the Three Governance Tiers

The guidelines sit in the middle of a three-tier structure. Understanding where you're operating helps you know which rules are fixed and which are yours to set.

Figure 1 — the AI Usage Policy Framework

Click each tier to see what sits inside it

These guidelines do not override POPIA, plagiarism policy, or exam regulations — they sit between institutional policy and your course-level rules, translating the former into practical direction and leaving room for the latter.

ApexInstitutional policies
POPIA, the UWC Plagiarism Policy, Assessment Regulations, UNESCO/OECD standards, and the UWC AI Guidelines (2025). These are non-negotiable — the Faculty guidelines cannot loosen them.
MiddleFaculty of Education AI Guidelines
Defines roles, accountability and practical toolkits for the Faculty — the document this whole course is built from. It interprets institutional policy for our specific teaching, learning, assessment and research contexts.
BaseCourse & Department-level policy
Assignment protocols, disclosure requirements and grading rubrics that you, as the lecturer or supervisor, set for your own module — consistent with the two tiers above.

Scope

The Faculty of Education spans three departments — Educational Studies, Language Education, Educational Psychology — plus the School of Science and Mathematics Education, the Institute for Post-School Studies, and the Business Science Education Cluster, across undergraduate to doctoral level. These guidelines apply everywhere teaching, learning, assessment and research happen: lectures, labs, fieldwork, tutorials.

Faculty aims for ethical AI use

2.1 Provide clear guidance
Faculty-specific direction on ethical, pedagogically sound AI use across generative AI, LLMs, predictive analytics and automated grading.
2.2 Safeguard academic integrity
Harness AI's benefits without compromising integrity — transparency, attribution and honesty in all AI-assisted work.
2.3 Align with policies & best practice
Consistency with UWC policy plus global best practice (UNESCO, OECD) adapted to the realities of the Global South.
2.4 Establish roles & governance
Clear roles, responsibilities and governance structures with ongoing oversight and review.
2.5 Support implementation
Practical tools — checklists, templates, sample policies — to embed the guidelines in everyday practice.
