Governance & Roles

Eight bodies share responsibility for AI governance in the Faculty. Knowing who owns what saves you from guessing — or from escalating to the wrong committee.

Click a role to expand its responsibilities

EDULT Committee

Faculty of Education Learning & Teaching Committee

Primary oversight body. 'AI in L&T' is a standing agenda item. Approves guideline revisions (with EDUHD), may approve new AI tools with ICS/CIECT, and interprets the guidelines in complex cases. Also considers assessment 'rules' — e.g. every module should include at least one in-person assessment.
CIECT

Centre for Innovative Education & Communication Technologies

Delivers the mandatory 'Ethical AI Use' course, runs workshops (prompt engineering, discipline-specific tools), and helps redesign curricula or assessments on request.
UWC Library

Library & AI LibGuide

Curates the AI LibGuide, runs information-literacy workshops on ethical AI scholarship and citation, and liaises with CIECT, the Writing Centre and RDPS.
ICS

Information & Communication Services

Vets and approves AI platforms, maintains infrastructure, monitors cybersecurity/data-protection issues, and reviews vendor agreements with Legal where needed.
CSSS

Centre for Student Support Services

Supports the bio-psycho-social side of AI use — vetting platforms for inclusion and safety, and providing counselling support for staff and students engaging with AI.
AI Champions

Departmental Nodal Persons

One staff and one student representative per Department. Handle training, feedback collection, reporting, case studies, and research encouragement (see Table 1).
Academic Staff

Lecturers, tutors, supervisors

Disclose AI assistance in materials/assessments, review AI-generated content for accuracy, and integrate AI literacy into pedagogy.
Students & Reps

Faculty Student Council, SRC, AI Champions

Beneficiaries and active partners — provide feedback, run peer initiatives, and sit on the Ad Hoc AI Task Team.

A living document

The Ad Hoc AI Task Team (one staff + one student per Department) meets each term to reflect on implementation and recommend changes to EDULT and EDUHD. Urgent changes can be communicated via Faculty Communications to the Dean. If you're unsure who owns a decision, this is the escalation path.
