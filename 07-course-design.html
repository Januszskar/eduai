Course Design & Delivery

Seven concrete moves for building AI consideration into your module — from outcomes alignment to labelling AI-generated materials.

Seven design moves

Align AI use with learning outcomes
Review your module outcomes and decide whether AI helps or hinders them. Design tasks that require conceptual understanding even when AI is available.
Reference the guidelines in your course outline
Cite the Faculty AI Guidelines explicitly so students know where to find the overarching policy and expectations.
Give explicit per-assessment instructions
For each assessment, state clearly when AI is permitted, restricted or prohibited, and what level of AI contribution is acceptable.
Use standard template language
Define, consistently across your outline, when and how AI may or may not be used.
Build AI into learning activities on purpose
Have students compare their own responses to AI-generated output, or critically evaluate AI-produced content as an exercise in itself.
Label AI-generated teaching materials
If you use AI to generate slides or quiz questions, mark them as such and review all content for accuracy before use.
Assume independent student AI use
Counter potential misuse with personal engagement elements — oral presentations, in-class assessments, reflective components — that reinforce mastery regardless of what happened outside class.

A practical starting point

Pick one assessment in your current module. Using the accordion above, decide: is AI permitted, restricted, or prohibited for it — and does your course outline actually say so yet?
