Exam rules that affect your module

Balance your modes of assessment
At least one assessment per module should be in-person — especially for CA modules. For CFA modules, the final exam should not be a purely online written submission; use a sit-down exam or alternatives like presentations or orals.
Disclosure before submission
Students complete an AI Checklist (a simple yes/no list) before submitting written work, addressing the task requirements.
Exam design that resists AI
Open-ended questions, multi-part problems and context-specific scenarios reduce the effectiveness of generic AI responses.
No AI or devices during invigilated exams
All electronic devices must be powered off and stored away; only explicitly approved calculators or devices may be used.
Device checks & forensics
Invigilators may inspect devices; random checks may occur, and in exceptional cases the University may conduct forensic reviews.

Consequences

Using AI or an unauthorised tool during an exam or test is a serious breach of academic integrity — typical penalties include a zero for the assessment and referral to the University disciplinary process.
