Citation format for AI tools

OpenAI. (2025). ChatGPT (Month Day version) [Large language model]. https://chat.openai.com
Ought. (2025). Elicit [AI research assistant]. https://elicit.org/
In-text prompt citation: (ChatGPT, 2025, prompt: “…”) with a corresponding reference-list entry.

Sample disclosure statement

“This assignment used ChatGPT to generate an initial draft of the introduction, which I then revised.”

Encourage students to be this specific: which tool, which part of the work, and what they did with the output afterward.
