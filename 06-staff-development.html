Staff Development & Your Obligations

Training isn't optional, and disclosure duties apply to staff as much as students. Here's what's expected of you specifically.

What's mandatory for you

Training

AI literacy training is mandatory for all new staff and forms part of probation. Existing staff are encouraged into CIECT workshops, and completion counts toward annual performance review.

Disclosure

You must follow the same ethical principles as students: disclose AI use in teaching, research and assessment documentation. If AI drafts an email or exam question, review it for quality and be transparent when appropriate.

Supervision MOUs

If you supervise, sign an MOU that covers AI

All staff should sign an MOU with students (and with editors) covering ethical AI use and protection of data and original ownership. You're also responsible for ensuring your students are trained in the affordances of the AI platforms relevant to their research and learning.

Where to find support

Training workshops
Regular sessions by CIECT, the Library, Writing Centre or Post-Graduate Office on AI tools, prompt engineering and pedagogy — including bias mitigation and data privacy.
Scholarship of teaching and learning
Communities of practice, forums and mini-conferences for staff to publish and share research on AI-enhanced pedagogy.
Short courses on supervision & AI
Where UWC offers short courses on supervision and AI ethics, staff should be encouraged — and in some instances required — to attend.
